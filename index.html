PRC 1 • Chapter 4 (Accruals) Level 3: Advanced Exam Level

At 1 Jan 2025, rent prepayment was Rs. 12,000. Rent paid during the year was Rs. 96,000. At 31 Dec 2025, rent accrued was Rs. 8,000. What is the rent expense to be charged in the Statement of Profit or Loss?

A
Rs. 116,000
B
Rs. 100,000
C
Rs. 92,000
D
Rs. 80,000
✅ Step-by-Step Working & Explanation:
Expense = Cash Paid (96,000) + Opening Prepayment (12,000) + Closing Accrual (8,000) = Rs. 116,000.
Roman Urdu Explanation
Opening prepayment picchle saal ka pay ho chuka tha lekin is saal ka expense hai, isliye add karenge. Closing accrual abhi pay nahi hua lekin is saal ka expense hai, isliye isko bhi add karenge.
